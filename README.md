# Career Campus — From College to Career

## Project Overview

**Career Campus** is a student-focused career guidance website designed to help college students explore career options, understand the skills required for different career paths, identify skill gaps, follow structured roadmaps, discover relevant projects and learning resources, and understand industry trends.

The project focuses on creating a clear, professional, and student-friendly user experience from college to career.

## Problem Statement

College students often find it difficult to understand which career paths are available to them, what skills are required for those careers, and how they can systematically prepare themselves.

Career information and learning resources are often scattered across different platforms. Career Campus brings these areas together into one organized platform.

## Project Objectives

- Help students explore different career paths.
- Provide information about skills required for different careers.
- Help students identify skill gaps.
- Provide structured career roadmaps.
- Recommend relevant projects and learning resources.
- Present industry insights and skills in demand.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly interface for students.

## Main Features

### Home Page
Introduces Career Campus and provides quick access to the main sections of the website.

### How It Works
Explains the Career Campus journey step by step:
1. Create Your Profile
2. Add Your Skills
3. Explore Careers
4. Identify Skill Gaps
5. Follow Your Roadmap
6. Build Projects

### Login / Sign Up
Provides the entry point for users to create an account or log in.

### About Your Skills
Allows users to provide their academic background, interests, current skills, and skill levels.

### Career Guide
Allows students to explore career paths and view career details, required skills, skill gaps, and career roadmaps.

### Projects & Resources
Provides project ideas and learning resources related to career preparation.

### Industry Insights
Presents information about industries, skills in demand, industry trends, and emerging career roles.

### About
Explains the purpose, vision, and concept behind Career Campus.

## Website Flow

**Home → Get Started → Login / Sign Up → Career Journey**

The navigation also provides direct access to:
- Home
- How It Works
- Career Guide

## UI/UX Design

The design of Career Campus follows a professional, modern, and student-friendly visual style.

### Color Palette

- **Dark Navy** — Primary brand and navigation color
- **Warm Cream / Off-White** — Background
- **White** — Cards and content areas
- **Muted Gold** — Accent color
- **Dark Charcoal** — Main text

### Design Principles

- Clean and organized layouts
- Consistent typography
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Card-based content organization
- Consistent buttons and navigation
- Adequate whitespace
- Responsive design considerations
- Student-friendly user experience
