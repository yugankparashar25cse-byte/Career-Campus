TECHNOLOGY

AI is Transforming Work

Artificial intelligence is changing how teams build products, analyze information, automate repetitive tasks, and solve complex problems. Students who understand AI tools and concepts will have a significant advantage entering the workforce.

Read Insight  →

WORKPLACE

Skills Are Becoming More Important

Employers increasingly value practical skills, problem-solving ability, adaptability, and continuous learning alongside academic knowledge. Demonstrating what you can build matters as much as your degree.

Read Insight  →

CAREER READINESS

Projects Build Career Readiness

Practical projects help students demonstrate skills, develop a portfolio, and show real-world problem-solving ability. A strong project portfolio makes a meaningful difference when applying for roles.
